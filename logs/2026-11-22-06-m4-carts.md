## Learned / surprises
- rtc3test logic passes, but the screenshots need CGB palette colors (green
  PASS). STATUS was wrong that the renderer drew it. CGB palette colors
  are pulled into M4 (35b7926), after the perf cuts.
- mbc3-tester reference PNG uses #7BFF4A; contract says #7BFF31 (verified
  by decoding the PNG). Escalated (Needs human).
- Rust doesn't propagate `#[must_use]` through `Option`: `emu.eject();` is
  now rejected, `emu.load(..).unwrap();` over a loaded cart is not (checked
  with rustc). Accepted as a documented limitation.
- ADR 0014's "reuse the look-ahead walk" cut measured slower; dropped.
- The orchestrator re-ran two of the advance_seconds mutations (agent was
  suspiciously fast): both failed as claimed.
