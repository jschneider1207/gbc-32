## Learned / surprises
- gambatte `timeToNextLy` T maps to our position as t = 7 - T on the next line.
- A second entry into the 256-arm opcode dispatch made LLVM duplicate the
  jump-table jump and keep `&regs.pc` in a register at 88 sites (+6.5 M Ir
  on worst-case). Route special opcodes through the single fetch point.
- Caching the `next_event` walk result for `sync` costs more than it saves
  (`next_event` becomes `&mut self`); `Hw` is exactly at its 864 B limit.
- Rebaselining a guard to pass the gate is a human decision in practice
  (the auto-mode classifier blocked it until the human approved).
