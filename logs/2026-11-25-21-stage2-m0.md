## Learned / surprises
- The ST-LINK MCO is 8.0644 MHz (+8,048 ppm, it wanders ±0.05%), so the HSE
  path ran the CPU at 483.86 MHz, over the limit. The PC timing (484.3)
  agreed with the LSE measurement.
- Waiting for VOSRDY right after ODEN passes immediately on the stale VOS1
  flag. The code waits for the drop, then the rise, then 100 µs.
- d3cr/csr1 can't show VOS0; only `oden=1` does. SYSCFG_PWRCR bit 7
  (reserved) sets itself after ODEN.
- With the same clock and sources, a rebuild moves cycles/frame by about ±2%
  (code layout). This matters for the planned +3% on-board ratchet (M6).
- `end pass=N` in h7-bench is a repeat counter, not a verdict. The first
  report after a flash is often truncated by the ST-LINK.
- The WSL clock ran ~2% slow against Windows. Don't time with it.
