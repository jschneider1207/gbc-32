## Learned / surprises
- The shield is the $AZ2 variant: **ST7789V**, not ILI9341V (RDDID 85 85 52,
  TE 60 Hz). Frame rate via FRCTRL2/PORCTRL, not FRMCTR1.
- ST7789V readback: 5-bit R/B expand to 6 bits with green's LSB (measured).
- FAST timing (hclk3 236 MHz, 16 cycles) works but violates hold times.
- MDMA transfers at exactly the FMC bus rate; FMC write FIFO keeps up; no
  measurable contention with the core's QSPI/flash reads.
- In plain TE mode the loop phase-locks so TE comes after the core's frame;
  `te-wait` forces overlap for the contention measurement.
